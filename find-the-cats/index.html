How to play

Every cat hides under a tile. Click tiles to uncover them and find all the cats.

  • No two cats share a row or a column.
  • No two cats touch, not even diagonally.
  • When you find a cat, its row, column and eight neighbors are crossed out. Those tiles are safe from cats and can’t be clicked.
  • Use the crossed-out tiles to work out where the remaining cats can be.
